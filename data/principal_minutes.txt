### Meeting Minutes: Student Success Weekly Meeting

**Title:** Student Success Weekly Meeting
**Attendees:** Not specified (Meeting participants)

**Agenda:**
*   Addressing the trend of Friday absenteeism.
*   Addressing the increase in student illness.
*   Reviewing the case of John Smith (chronic absenteeism).

**Discussion:**
*   **Friday Absenteeism:** The team noted a trend of students skipping school on Fridays. Reasons discussed included difficulty waking up and general lack of motivation. A proposal was made to host a pancake breakfast to incentivize attendance.
*   **Student Health:** With the onset of colder weather, there has been an increase in students reporting symptoms of illness (coughing, sniffling). The team discussed the importance of preventative hygiene education.
*   **John Smith (Case Study):** John has missed seven days of school this year. It was reported that he is experiencing significant stress due to childcare responsibilities for his younger siblings. The team agreed that he needs additional support and guidance.

**Decisions:**
*   **Friday Attendance:** The team approved a pilot "Pancake Breakfast" to be held next week to encourage Friday attendance.
*   **Health Awareness:** The team decided to create and display posters regarding flu season prevention and hygiene (e.g., handwashing).
*   **John Smith Support:** The team decided to refer John to the guidance counselor and provide his family with information regarding local, low-cost childcare resources.

**Action Items:**
*   **Pancake Breakfast:** Organize and host a pancake breakfast for next Friday.
*   **Health Posters:** Create and post hygiene/flu prevention posters throughout the school.
*   **Guidance Referral:** Facilitate a meeting between John Smith and the guidance counselor.
*   **Resource Research:** Identify and compile a list of free/low-cost community childcare resources to share with John and his family.
