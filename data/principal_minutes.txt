### Meeting Minutes: Fair Threads Senior Management Team

**Title:** Senior Management Meeting – Remote Working Policy Proposal
**Date:** [Insert Date]
**Location:** Fair Threads Office

**Attendees:**
*   **Samantha Mason:** Chief Executive Officer (Chair)
*   **Alex:** Head of Information
*   **Rachel:** Administration
*   **John:** Head of Finance
*   **Matthew:** Head of Research

---

**Agenda:**
1. Proposal for a formal Work-From-Home (WFH) policy.
2. Review of the Strategic Plan (Briefly introduced).

---

**Discussion:**
*   **Proposal Background:** Matthew presented a request from his team for a formal WFH policy. The team feels the current office environment is too busy/noisy for deep-focus work (writing) and wishes to move away from the current "ad hoc" approach to avoid feeling guilty.
*   **Management Perspectives:**
    *   **John (Finance):** Expressed skepticism regarding costs, equipment provision, and potential insurance/liability risks.
    *   **Alex (Information):** Supported the proposal, noting that most staff already have necessary equipment and that WFH would benefit working parents and improve productivity on large projects.
    *   **Rachel (Administration):** Opposed the policy, noting that it is not feasible for all departments (e.g., admin). She warned that an inconsistent policy could alienate staff and create perceptions of unfairness.
    *   **Samantha (CEO):** Acknowledged the office overcrowding and noise issues but emphasized the need to balance staff flexibility with financial reality and departmental fairness.
*   **Conclusion:** The board agreed that while there are significant concerns regarding equity and liability, the proposal warrants further investigation to remain competitive and support staff well-being.

---

**Decisions:**
*   The proposal for a formal WFH policy will not be rejected at this stage; it will be moved to a research phase.
*   Matthew and John are tasked with conducting a formal review of the proposal to address financial and operational concerns.

---

**Action Items:**
*   **Research & Analysis:** Matthew and John to research WFH practices in similar organizations, specifically focusing on:
    *   Cost implications.
    *   Insurance and Health & Safety liabilities.
*   **Joint Presentation:** Matthew and John to prepare and deliver a joint presentation on their findings at the next meeting.
*   **Scheduling:** Matthew and John to coordinate a meeting date prior to the next board meeting to finalize their report.
*   **Next Meeting:** Scheduled for a fortnight from today.
