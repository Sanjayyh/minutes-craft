#### **Agenda**
1.  Proposal for a formal Work-From-Home (WFH) policy.
2.  Review of the Strategic Plan.

---

#### **Discussion**
*   **Proposal Overview:** Matthew presented a request from his team for a formal WFH policy. The team feels the current office environment is too busy and distracting for project-based writing tasks, and they desire a formal policy to avoid the "guilt" associated with ad-hoc arrangements.
*   **Arguments in Favor:** 
    *   **Alex:** Supports the idea, noting that many staff already have the necessary equipment. He highlighted the benefits for working parents and the potential for reduced stress during large projects.
    *   **Matthew:** Emphasized that flexible, family-friendly working is a competitive necessity in their industry and that rejecting the proposal outright would negatively impact staff morale.
    *   **Management:** Acknowledged that the office is currently overcrowded and noisy, making it a difficult work environment.
*   **Arguments Against/Concerns:**
    *   **John:** Expressed skepticism regarding the necessity of a policy, citing concerns over costs, equipment provision, and potential insurance/liability implications.
    *   **Rachel:** Raised concerns regarding fairness and equity, noting that her administrative team cannot perform their duties remotely. She warned that a partial policy could alienate staff members who are required to be on-site.
*   **Management Stance:** Samantha noted the difficulty of implementing a policy that is fair across all departments while maintaining financial realism.

---

#### **Decisions**
*   The proposal for a formal WFH policy will not be rejected at this time; instead, it will be subject to further investigation.
*   A joint research project will be conducted to assess the feasibility, costs, and risks of the proposal.

---

#### **Action Items**
*   **Matthew and John:** Collaborate to research the proposal further, specifically focusing on:
    *   Benchmarking other organizations that have implemented similar policies.
    *   Analyzing the financial costs involved.
    *   Investigating insurance and health and safety liabilities.
*   **Matthew and John:** Prepare and deliver a joint presentation of their findings at the next meeting.
