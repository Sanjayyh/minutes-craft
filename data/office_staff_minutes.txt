### Meeting Minutes: Student Success Weekly Meeting

**Title:** Student Success Weekly Meeting
**Attendees:** Not specified (Team members present)
**Agenda:** 
*   Addressing trends in chronic absenteeism (specifically Fridays)
*   Addressing seasonal illness among students
*   Reviewing the case of John Smith (chronic absenteeism)

**Discussion:**
*   **Friday Absenteeism:** The team noted a trend of students skipping school on Fridays. Mentors reported that students find it difficult to wake up for school at the end of the week. The team discussed incentivizing attendance with a pancake breakfast.
*   **Seasonal Illness:** With the onset of colder weather, there has been an increase in students reporting symptoms of illness (coughing, sniffling). The team discussed the need for preventative health education.
*   **John Smith Case:** John has missed seven days of school this year. It was reported that he is experiencing significant stress due to childcare responsibilities for his younger siblings. The team agreed that he needs additional support and guidance.

**Decisions:**
*   **Friday Attendance:** A pancake breakfast will be hosted next week to encourage Friday attendance.
*   **Health Awareness:** Educational posters regarding hygiene and illness prevention will be displayed throughout the school.
*   **John Smith Support:** John will be referred to the guidance counselor for support, and the team will provide his family with information on local, low-cost childcare resources.

**Action Items:**
*   **Pancake Breakfast:** Organize and host a pancake breakfast for next Friday.
*   **Health Posters:** Create and post signage regarding flu season and hygiene tips (e.g., handwashing).
*   **Guidance Referral:** Facilitate a meeting between John Smith and the guidance counselor.
*   **Resource Research:** Identify and compile a list of free/low-cost community childcare resources to share with John and his family.
