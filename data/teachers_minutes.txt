### Meeting Minutes: Student Success Weekly Meeting

**Title:** Student Success Weekly Meeting
**Attendees:** Not specified (Team members present)
**Agenda:** 
*   Addressing trends in chronic absenteeism (specifically Fridays).
*   Addressing the increase in student illness.
*   Reviewing the case of John Smith (chronic absenteeism).

---

### Discussion
*   **Friday Absenteeism:** The team noted a trend of students skipping school on Fridays. Mentees reported difficulty getting out of bed as a primary factor.
*   **Student Illness:** With the onset of colder weather, there has been an increase in students exhibiting symptoms of illness (coughing, sniffling).
*   **John Smith Case:** John has missed seven days of school this year. It was reported that he is under significant stress due to family responsibilities, specifically caring for younger siblings during the day.

### Decisions
*   **Friday Attendance:** The team will host a pancake breakfast next week to incentivize Friday attendance.
*   **John Smith Support:** The team agreed that John should meet with the guidance counselor to discuss his stress and family situation.

### Action Items
*   **Health Awareness:** Create and post informational posters regarding flu season prevention (e.g., handwashing tips) for students and staff.
*   **Counseling Referral:** Facilitate a meeting between John Smith and the guidance counselor.
*   **Resource Research:** Identify and compile a list of free or low-cost local childcare resources to provide to John and his family.
