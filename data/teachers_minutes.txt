### Agenda
1.  Proposal for a formal Work-From-Home (WFH) policy.
2.  Review of the Strategic Plan (Briefly introduced).

---

### Discussion
*   **Proposal Overview:** Matthew presented a request from his team to formalize a WFH policy. The team currently finds the office environment too noisy for deep-focus tasks (writing) and feels guilty working from home on an ad-hoc basis.
*   **Arguments in Favor:**
    *   **Flexibility & Retention:** Alex noted that WFH supports employees with families and reduces stress for project-based work. Matthew emphasized that flexible, family-friendly working is a competitive necessity in their industry.
    *   **Office Environment:** The CEO acknowledged that the office is currently overcrowded and noisy, making it a difficult work environment.
*   **Arguments Against/Concerns:**
    *   **Fairness:** Rachel expressed concern that a WFH policy would alienate departments (like Administration) where physical presence is mandatory, creating an inequitable workplace culture.
    *   **Financials:** John raised concerns regarding the lack of cost analysis and potential equipment/infrastructure expenses.
    *   **Risk & Liability:** John highlighted significant health and safety/insurance implications regarding injuries sustained while working from home.

---

### Decisions
*   The proposal will not be rejected at this stage to maintain staff morale.
*   The management team agreed that further research is required to address the financial, legal, and equity concerns before a final decision can be made.

---

### Action Items
*   **Research & Analysis:** Matthew and John are to collaborate on a formal research project regarding the WFH proposal.
    *   *Scope:* Investigate industry benchmarks (other organizations), insurance/liability implications, and comprehensive costings.
*   **Presentation:** Matthew and John to deliver a joint presentation of their findings at the next senior management meeting.
*   **Scheduling:** Matthew and John to coordinate a meeting date prior to the next management session to prepare their presentation.
