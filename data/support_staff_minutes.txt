### Meeting Minutes: Student Success Weekly Meeting

**Title:** Student Success Weekly Meeting
**Attendees:** Not specified (Team Members)
**Agenda:** 
*   Addressing trends in chronic absenteeism (specifically Fridays).
*   Addressing seasonal illness concerns.
*   Reviewing the case of John Smith (chronic absenteeism).

---

### Discussion
*   **Friday Absenteeism:** The team noted a trend of students missing school on Fridays. Mentors reported that students find it difficult to wake up for school at the end of the week.
*   **Seasonal Illness:** With the onset of colder weather, there has been an increase in students reporting symptoms like coughing and sniffling. The team discussed the need for preventative health reminders.
*   **Case Study: John Smith:** John has missed seven days of school as of November. It was reported that he is experiencing significant stress due to childcare responsibilities for his younger siblings. The team agreed that he needs additional support to balance his home life and education.

---

### Decisions
*   **Friday Attendance:** The team approved a "Pancake Breakfast" initiative to be held next week to incentivize Friday attendance.
*   **John Smith Support:** The team decided to refer John to the guidance counselor for support and to provide his family with resources for childcare.

---

### Action Items
*   **Health Awareness:** Create and post informational posters throughout the school regarding flu season prevention (e.g., handwashing tips).
*   **Counseling Referral:** Facilitate a meeting between John Smith and the guidance counselor.
*   **Resource Gathering:** Research and compile a list of free or low-cost community childcare resources to provide to John Smith and his family.
