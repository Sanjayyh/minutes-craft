**Agenda:**
1.  Proposal for a formal Work-From-Home (WFH) policy.
2.  Review of the Strategic Plan.

---

**Discussion:**
*   **Proposal Background:** Matthew presented a request from his team for a formal WFH policy. The team finds the current office environment too busy for deep-focus work (writing) and feels guilty working from home on an ad-hoc basis.
*   **Management Perspectives:**
    *   **Samantha (CEO):** Acknowledged office overcrowding and noise issues but expressed concern regarding the necessity of a formal policy versus the current flexible arrangement.
    *   **John (Finance):** Raised concerns regarding costs, equipment provision, and potential insurance/liability implications.
    *   **Alex (IT):** Supported the proposal, noting that most staff already have the necessary equipment and that WFH would benefit working parents and improve project focus.
    *   **Rachel (Admin):** Opposed the proposal, citing fairness concerns as her team’s duties require physical presence in the office.
*   **Strategic Context:** Matthew emphasized that flexible, family-friendly working is a competitive necessity in their industry and that rejecting the proposal outright would negatively impact staff morale.

---

**Decisions:**
*   The proposal will not be rejected at this stage; further research is required to address financial and operational viability.
*   A joint presentation on the feasibility of a formal WFH policy will be delivered at the next meeting.

---

**Action Items:**
*   **Matthew & John:** Conduct further research into the proposal, specifically focusing on:
    *   Benchmarking other organizations with similar practices.
    *   Detailed cost analysis.
    *   Insurance, health, and safety implications.
*   **Matthew & John:** Schedule a preparatory meeting to coordinate the joint presentation.
*   **All:** Review the Strategic Plan papers in preparation for the next meeting.
